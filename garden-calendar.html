Calendar

A garden year at a glance

Timings suit temperate climates. Adjust by a month or two for your region.

Mar to May

Sow seeds, divide perennials and feed lawns.

Jun to Aug

Water deeply, deadhead flowers and watch for pests.

Sep to Nov

Plant bulbs, rake leaves and collect seeds.

Dec to Feb

Prune dormant plants, protect pots and plan next year.
