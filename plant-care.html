Plant care in four steps

The same four basics keep most plants healthy.

1. Light

Match the plant to the light. Bright and indirect suits most houseplants, full sun suits most flowers.

2. Water

Check the top two centimetres of soil. Water when dry and let extra water drain away.

3. Soil

Use a well-draining mix. Pots need drainage holes so the roots do not sit in water.

4. Feed

Use a balanced fertiliser during the growing season and pause in winter.
