About

A guide built for people who love green things.

Petal & Root brings seasonal flower knowledge and simple plant care together in one calm place.

Our approach

Every page is written in plain language. Each plant has a short profile, and each season has its own colours so you always know where you are.

The site is built with clean HTML and CSS, so it loads fast and works on any device.

Made to be reused

The layout is a template. A nursery, florist, garden cafe or landscaping studio can replace the name, colours and plant lists to launch a branded site. Online ordering can be added later as an extra section.

Meet the owner

Petal & Root is created and owned by Laiba Noor. It is her seasonal guide to flowers, plant care and indoor greenery, designed to be simple, calm and easy to use.
