Language of flowers

What your bouquet says

Flowers have carried messages for centuries. These meanings are traditional and vary by culture.

Rosa

Rose

Love and appreciation. Red for romance, yellow for friendship.

Helianthus

Sunflower

Warmth, loyalty and happiness.

Lavandula

Lavender

Calm and devotion.

Bellis perennis

Daisy

Innocence and new beginnings.

Tulipa

Tulip

Perfect love and a fresh start.

Jasminum

Jasmine

Grace and sweet affection.
